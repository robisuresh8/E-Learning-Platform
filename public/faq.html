Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses, enrollment, and platform

What is ICTRD LEARNING? +

ICTRD LEARNING is an online education platform that offers comprehensive courses designed to help you advance your career. We provide expert-led training, hands-on projects, and industry-recognized certificates across various fields including technology, business, design, and more.

How do I enroll in a course? +

To enroll in a course, simply create a free account, browse our course catalog, and click "Enroll Now" on any course that interests you. You can start learning immediately after enrollment.

Do I get a certificate after completing a course? +

Yes! Upon successful completion of a course, including all assignments and assessments, you'll receive an industry-recognized certificate that you can add to your resume and LinkedIn profile.

How long do I have access to course materials? +

Once you enroll in a course, you have lifetime access to all course materials, including video lectures, resources, and updates. You can learn at your own pace and revisit the content anytime.

Are the courses suitable for beginners? +

Absolutely! We offer courses for all skill levels - from complete beginners to advanced professionals. Each course clearly indicates its difficulty level, and many courses start with fundamentals before progressing to advanced topics.

Can I learn on mobile devices? +

Yes, our platform is fully responsive and works seamlessly on desktop computers, tablets, and mobile phones. You can access your courses and learn on the go from any device with an internet connection.

What if I need help during a course? +

We provide multiple support channels including course discussion forums where you can ask questions, direct messaging with instructors, and our dedicated support team. You're never alone in your learning journey!

Do you offer refunds? +

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with your course within the first 30 days, you can request a full refund - no questions asked.

Are there any prerequisites for courses? +

Prerequisites vary by course. Beginner courses typically require no prior knowledge, while advanced courses may require specific skills or completion of prerequisite courses. All prerequisites are clearly listed on each course page.

How do I track my learning progress? +

Your dashboard provides detailed progress tracking including completion percentages, time spent learning, quiz scores, and project submissions. You can see exactly where you are in each course and what's coming next.
