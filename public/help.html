Getting Started

How do I create an account? +

Creating an account is easy! Click the "Sign Up" button in the top navigation, fill in your details, and verify your email address. Once verified, you can start enrolling in courses immediately.

How do I enroll in a course? +

Browse our course catalog, click on any course that interests you, and click the "Enroll Now" button. You'll have immediate access to all course materials and can start learning right away.

Can I preview a course before enrolling? +

Yes! Most courses offer free preview lessons. Click on any course to view the course details page where you can watch preview videos and read the full curriculum before making a decision.

Billing & Payments

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our encrypted payment gateway.

What is your refund policy? +

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with your course within the first 30 days, contact our support team for a full refund - no questions asked.

Do you offer payment plans? +

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans for select courses. You can split the cost into monthly installments. Check the course page for available payment plan options.

Course Content

How long do I have access to course materials? +

Once you enroll, you have lifetime access to all course materials, including video lectures, downloadable resources, and any future updates to the course content.

Can I download course materials? +

Yes! Most courses include downloadable resources such as PDFs, code files, templates, and other materials. These are available in the course dashboard for each lesson.

How do I track my progress? +

Your course dashboard shows detailed progress including completion percentage, time spent, quiz scores, and project submissions. You can always see where you are in each course.
